Why Donate Blood?

Blood can't be manufactured — it can only come from generous donors like you.

💛

Save up to 3 Lives

One whole blood donation is separated into red cells, plasma, and platelets — each saving a different patient.

📅

Donate Every 56 Days

Your body replenishes red blood cells within weeks. You can donate whole blood every 8 weeks, platelets every 7 days.

🏥

Free Health Checkup

Each donation includes a free mini health screening: blood pressure, pulse, hemoglobin, and disease testing.

🎯

Quick & Safe

The entire donation process takes about 45–60 minutes. The actual blood draw takes only 8–10 minutes.

📈

Heart Health Benefit

Regular donation may help reduce iron overload in the blood, potentially reducing the risk of heart disease.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about blood donation.

Who can donate blood? ⌄
Generally, you can donate if you are between 17–65 years old, weigh at least 50 kg, are in good health, and haven't donated in the last 56 days. Use our Eligibility Checker to find out for sure.
Does donating blood hurt? ⌄
Most donors feel a quick pinch when the needle is inserted, then little or no discomfort during the 8–10 minute donation. You may feel slightly light-headed afterward, which passes quickly with water and a light snack.
How long does the full process take? ⌄
Plan for about 45–60 minutes total: registration (10 min) + health history & mini physical (10 min) + donation (10 min) + refreshments & rest (15 min). First-time donors may take slightly longer.
How often can I donate? ⌄
Whole blood: every 56 days (8 weeks). Platelets: every 7 days, up to 24 times per year. Plasma: every 28 days. Double red cells: every 112 days.
What should I do before donating? ⌄
Drink plenty of water the day before and day of donation. Eat a healthy meal beforehand (avoid fatty foods). Get a good night's sleep. Wear comfortable clothing with sleeves that can be rolled up.
